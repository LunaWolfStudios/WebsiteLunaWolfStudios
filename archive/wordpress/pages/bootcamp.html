Overview

At Luna Wolf Studios, we believe in empowering the next generation of game developers to create meaningful, immersive experiences. Whether you’re just starting your journey or ready to gain real studio experience, we invite you to run with the pack and take your first steps into the world of game development.

Remote Internship Program (Unpaid)

For aspiring developers ready to go hands-on in a real studio environment.

Our Internship Program offers a unique, project based experience where interns collaborate in small teams to bring a game from concept to demo release.

Under the guidance of our seasoned developers, you’ll learn through doing, using industry standard tools, workflows, and best practices. Expect weekly team meetings and day-to-day communication, giving you a glimpse into how real studios operate.

This is a hands-off, self-driven opportunity. You’ll get out of it what you put in. The experience is perfect for students who have already built their own games and are seeking true industry experience and mentorship.

Interns may also be eligible for college course credit (depending on institution policies).

This Internship typically runs between late May and mid August.

Remote Introductory GameDev Bootcamp

For beginners ready to start their journey into game development.

If you’ve ever dreamed of building your own video game but didn’t know where to start, this is where your journey begins.

This bootcamp is a hands-on course taught by industry professionals. Together, you’ll explore the full creative process of making a game, from concept to prototype, while learning the tools and techniques used by real studios.

You’ll learn:
•  Game design fundamentals and theory
•  How to build and code games using Unity and C#
•  Visuals, audio, and narrative integration
•  Production planning and source control (Git)
•  Industry standard best practices for game development

This course is perfect for beginners, career changers, or hobbyists looking to turn creativity into skill. You’ll also join our growing developer community (“the pack”), where you can share ideas, get feedback, and build lasting industry connections.

By the end of our introductory bootcamp, you’ll have the confidence and skills to design and build your own games from scratch.

This is a 10 week remote bootcamp. You are required to have your own laptop or computer capable of running Unity. There will be a weekly class with hands-on instructions as well as assigned work throughout each week.

Email support@lunawolfstudios.com to reserve your spot for our Summer Bootcamp. A minimum deposit will be required to reserve your spot.
