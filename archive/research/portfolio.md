  word. Nothing from outside the old site is used for roles. Where a founder page gives only a
  credit line ("by Peloton"), that credit line is the whole statement.

  row and the sources listed under each table.
- **Listed by:** `jeff` = `about-jeff.md`, `jeremy` = `about-jeremy.md` (the regenerated
  17,379-byte file), `olivia` = `about-olivia.md`, `studio` = `games.md` or the Home page
  (`game-design-and-development.md`).
- **Link status** was checked on 2026-11-21 with curl (generic browser User-Agent) and, for
  script-rendered or bot-blocked pages, headless Edge. "BOT-BLOCKED" means the site refuses
  automated clients; existence was confirmed another way, named in the cell.
