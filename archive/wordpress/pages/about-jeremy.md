### Jeremy Bregermann

**Co-Founder & Lead Game Designer, Luna Wolf Studios LLC**

_Game Developer • Unity & Unreal Engineer • University Professor • AI-Assisted Development Specialist_

I’m Jeremy Bregermann, a game designer, software developer, educator, and co-founder of Luna Wolf Studios LLC. With over 11 years of professional game development experience, I’ve worked across commercial games, independent projects, interactive simulations, virtual and augmented reality, educational software, mobile applications, and emerging technologies.

My experience spans the entire game development lifecycle, from initial concept and gameplay design through programming, prototyping, debugging, quality assurance, optimization, publishing, and post-release support.

At Luna Wolf Studios, I’ve helped develop, fund, and publish projects including **HELIX**, a gamified virtual reality hearing assessment developed in collaboration with Nemours Children’s Hospital; **Sheep Tag 2**, a Kickstarter-funded 3D online multiplayer real-time strategy game; and **The Brave Hearts II: False Skies**, released on Steam in August 2026.

I’ve also contributed to educational games for Legends of Learning, rehabilitation software including Flex Sledding for the mTrigger, and independent games such as Haberdashery. Beyond traditional games, I’ve created interactive training applications, mobile software, websites, videos, digital marketing campaigns, and custom software solutions for clients.

## Game Development, Engineering & AI

My primary development expertise is in **Unity and C#**, alongside experience with Unreal Engine 5, C++, Python, JavaScript, and a wide range of development tools.

I specialize in gameplay systems, physics, AI behaviors, user interfaces, game controllers, scene management, networking, multiplayer, game saves, procedural content, and third-party integrations.

I began developing games long before generative AI became part of modern software development. That traditional programming foundation remains central to how I design, evaluate, and deliver software.

Today, I also incorporate AI development assistants, including **OpenAI ChatGPT and Codex**, into my production workflows.

I use AI coding agents to accelerate implementation, refactoring, debugging, automated testing, documentation, and prototyping. I establish the creative vision, technical requirements, architectural direction, development constraints, and acceptance criteria, while overseeing implementation and evaluating results.

My work increasingly focuses on effective human-directed AI development, including creating reproducible technical challenges, identifying failures, testing complex gameplay interactions, and verifying that implemented solutions meet their intended requirements.

The result is a workflow that combines traditional engineering expertise with the speed and flexibility of modern AI-assisted production.

## Current Projects & Recent Releases

### Rowan of Bramblebrook — Playable Prototype / In Development

[Play Rowan of Bramblebrook on itch.io](https://bregermann.itch.io/rowan-of-bramblebrook)

An ambitious PS1-inspired 3D fantasy adventure combining platforming, exploration, combat, village life, relationships, romance, shopkeeping, and RPG systems.

Originally created for the Official Unity Game Jam 2026, Rowan of Bramblebrook began as a seven-day development challenge and has expanded into a larger game project.

I serve as the project’s sole human developer, handling game design, creative direction, world building, asset integration, production, testing, and technical oversight, with extensive assistance from ChatGPT and Codex.

The downloadable Windows prototype demonstrates a functioning interconnected world, NPC interactions, economy systems, weather, combat, cinematics, and a multi-phase dragon boss encounter.

**Technology:** Unity 6, C#, URP, AI-assisted development, gameplay systems, automated testing.

### Interstate: Hauler — Trucking Simulator — In Development

A large-scale trucking and life simulation project centered on driving, exploration, vehicle interactions, and life on the road.

The project combines vehicle physics, realistic driving controls, AI traffic, roads and highways, navigation, weather, dialogue, character interactions, save systems, and interactive locations.

Development includes controller-first gameplay, optional steering-wheel peripherals, and an expanding interconnected game world with supporting simulation systems.

**Technology:** Unity 6, C#, vehicle physics, AI, GPS/navigation, environment systems, third-party SDK integration.

### Truck Sports: Party! — In Development

A chaotic, physics-driven multiplayer party game built around competing in unconventional sports using trucks.

The project features multiple sporting events, local multiplayer, split-screen functionality, AI opponents, physics-driven gameplay, scoring systems, player profiles, and comprehensive gamepad support.

My development work focuses on reusable gameplay architecture, modular sporting events, multiplayer interactions, input systems, and streamlined player experiences.

**Technology:** Unity, C#, physics simulation, multiplayer architecture, AI, controller systems.

### The Brave Hearts II: False Skies — Published on Steam, 2026

[View The Brave Hearts II: False Skies on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4918650/)

A fully voice-acted, story-driven, top-down arcade shoot-’em-up developed by Connor Butler and published by Luna Wolf Studios.

Released August 27, 2026.

My contributions include Steam publishing and platform integration, Windows build verification, Steam achievements, Steam Cloud configuration, controller usability, and release quality assurance.

### Haberdashery — Modernization in Development

[Play Haberdashery on itch.io](https://lunawolfstudios.itch.io/haberdashery)

An award-nominated arcade endless runner starring a sheep collecting hats while navigating a rotating world filled with hazards.

Originally developed by Luna Wolf Studios, Haberdashery is being revisited with modern Unity technology, updated rendering, code modernization, improved compatibility, and gameplay refinements.

### Universal PS1 Game Asset Importer — Technical R&D

An ongoing Unity Editor tooling project focused on analyzing and importing legacy PlayStation-era game assets into modern real-time development environments.

The project explores modular import pipelines, game-specific adapters, geometry reconstruction, textures, collision information, scene generation, and diagnostic tools.

Related technical research includes rebuilding legacy environments as interactive Unity scenes, exploring original level structures, and developing debugging and visualization tools.

This is a research and tooling project rather than a commercial release of third-party game content.

### Dead Air — Game Jam Prototype, 2026

[View Dead Air on itch.io](https://bregermann.itch.io/dead-air)

A short atmospheric trucking horror game created for Brackeys Game Jam 2026.2.

Players navigate a lonely highway at night as radio transmissions and unsettling events turn a routine delivery into a psychological horror experience.

The project explores narrative events, vehicle interactions, audio-driven storytelling, environmental triggers, and branching outcomes.
